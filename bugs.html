Report a bug

FM X64 is an early preview, and every report helps. Found something that doesn't work, looks wrong or sounds wrong? Let us know.

Report on GitHub Report on Discord See open reports

GitHub needs a free account; on Discord you can just post your report.

What helps us fix it

  1. Your FM X64 version, shown at the top right of the launcher.
  2. What happened, and what you expected instead.
  3. How to make it happen: the steps that lead there, and whether it happens every time or only sometimes.
  4. A screenshot or short video if it's something you can see.
  5. Your PC: Windows version, CPU and graphics card.
  6. Your settings if they might matter: upscaling, frame generation, widescreen menus, monitors, frame rate.
  7. The log file fm1.log from the data folder in your FM X64 folder. It is rewritten each time you play, so copy it right after the problem happens.

Please don't send game files or anything from the game folder. The log can contain folder paths that include your Windows user name; you can replace it before sending.

Before you report

Some problems have a quick fix: see Problems on the install page. Performance depends on your hardware and may vary between PCs; if the game runs slowly, try a lower internal resolution or the 30 fps mode, and include your PC's specs in your report.
