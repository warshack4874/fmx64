This update finishes the paint shop fixes, sharpens replays and cleans up the widescreen menus. A note on the number first: 0.1.41 is simply the version after 0.1.4. From here versions count up as 0.1.42, 0.1.43 and so on.

## Painted cars, finally right

0.1.3 fixed the worst of the "static" on painted cars, but checking it again on a tester's own car showed two problems left over:

- window pillars and trim could take on the car's paint colour (red pillars on a Civic Type-R, for example);
- at any resolution above 1x, decals and badges got coloured fringes and garbled letters.

Both are fixed. The game saves the finished paint job, so **if one of your cars was painted while it looked wrong, open Apply Decals for it, change anything and save again.**

## Sharper replays

The depth-of-field blur in replay cameras blurred the whole picture and looked blocky. Now the car being followed is sharp and only the background and foreground blur, as intended.

## Cleaner widescreen menus

The remaining seams in the widescreen menus option are fixed, including the dark boxes and lines beside the Assists dialog and the step in the bottom status bar.

## For benchmarkers

`fm1_launcher --bench <track> --solo` now races your car alone, which gives much steadier numbers than a race with AI opponents.

If you have 0.1.1 or later, the launcher offers the update itself under **Updates**. After updating, it prepares the game once more, which takes about a minute.
