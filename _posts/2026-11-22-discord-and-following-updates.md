FM X64 now has a few easy ways to keep up with new versions and to help make it better.

## The Discord server

Come hang out, share screenshots and clips, ask for help with installing, and see work in progress: **[join the FM X64 Discord](https://discord.gg/Y23UNZnCue)**. New releases and posts like this one are announced there automatically.

## Found a bug?

Use the **[Report a bug](https://warshack4874.github.io/fmx64/bugs.html)** page. It explains what helps most (your version, the steps that cause it, and the `fm1.log` file) and has a short form on GitHub, or you can post your report on Discord.

## Following updates

- **In the launcher:** from 0.1.1 on, FM X64 checks for new versions by itself and installs them for you.
- **On Discord:** releases and website posts are posted automatically.
- **RSS:** add [the update feed](https://warshack4874.github.io/fmx64/feed.xml) to any RSS reader.
- **YouTube:** watch the [showcase video](https://youtu.be/qNC1_A8fA7c).

Thanks to everyone testing so far. Your reports have already led to five updates.
