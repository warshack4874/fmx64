Thanks to our first tester for a great bug report: after pausing a race and going back, all sound except the music could disappear. That is fixed in 0.1.1, along with a fresh look for the launcher.

## Fixed

- **Race sounds after pausing.** Engines, tires and the other cars could go silent after the pause menu (about half of all pauses). The cause was in our replacement of the Xbox sound system: it told the game that its 3D sound mixers were always busy, so the game slowly ran out of them. Fixed and tested in stereo and 5.1.

## New

- **A new launcher** in the same dark style as this site, with clear settings, tooltips and keyboard navigation.
- **Updates from the launcher.** FM X64 now checks for new versions (you can turn this off), shows what's new and installs the update for you, keeping your game, saves and settings.
- **"Graphics path" (experimental).** A first step towards drawing the game through its own Direct3D calls instead of emulating the Xbox graphics chip. It looks the same as before; "Emulated" stays the default.
