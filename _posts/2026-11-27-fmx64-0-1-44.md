FM X64 can now draw the game with **Vulkan** as well as Direct3D 11. Pick it in the launcher under **Graphics > Renderer**. Direct3D 11 stays the default.

- Vulkan draws everything: menus, videos, races, widescreen menus, monitor spanning and the benchmark. Menu pictures match Direct3D 11 pixel for pixel.
- On our test PC it runs at the same speed as Direct3D 11. So far it has only been tested on an AMD card, so if you have NVIDIA or Intel, please give it a try and let us know on Discord or through [Report a bug]({{ "/bugs.html" | relative_url }}).
- With Vulkan, upscaling uses FSR 1. FSR 4 and frame generation still need Direct3D 11, and the launcher switches between them for you.
- If Vulkan can't start on your PC, the game simply falls back to Direct3D 11.

If you have 0.1.1 or later, the launcher offers the update itself under **Updates**. After updating, it prepares the game once more, which takes about a minute.
