## Found a bug?

There's now a [Report a bug]({{ site.baseurl }}/bugs.html) page. Reports go to [GitHub](https://github.com/warshack4874/fmx64/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) through a short form, or to the [Discord server](https://discord.gg/Y23UNZnCue) if you don't have a GitHub account. The page explains what helps most, such as your version, the steps to make it happen and the `fm1.log` file.
