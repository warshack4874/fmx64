A small update with two fixes.

## Light glows

Taillights, lit signs and windows glow through the game's bloom effect. Its first blur step uses a special texture filter of the Xbox graphics chip, a 3x3 blur, which FM X64 drew as a plain lookup that skipped half the detail. Small and distant lights could flicker out of the glow, and glows looked patchy. FM X64 now does that blur the way the Xbox does, so glows are smoother and steadier. It shows most at night on Tokyo Circuit.

## Shortcuts

Installing through the setup window never actually created the Start menu and desktop shortcuts. That is fixed, and the installer now lets you choose: "Create a desktop shortcut" and "Add to the Start menu" are both on by default. If you used the zip or skipped them, the launcher can make them later under **Repair / re-import**.

If you have 0.1.1 or later, the launcher offers the update itself under **Updates**.
