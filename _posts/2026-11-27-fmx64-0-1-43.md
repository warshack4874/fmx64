A quick follow-up to 0.1.42, mostly for the experimental 16-car fields.

## Fixed

- **No control when starting 9th or lower in a 16-car race.** The game only got the first 8 grid places ready to drive each frame, so cars further back, including yours, could sit still at the green. Everyone now drives from the start.
- One car could be placed in the middle of the front rows instead of its own grid slot.
- Some cars had no engine sound in 16-car races: the game ran out of car sounds. There are now enough for the whole field.
- Race Winnings showed boxes instead of the place for 9th to 16th.
- **Single Race:** results after watching a replay could list a car twice; point-to-point roads now use each track's real start and finish (the road ended early before); clearer descriptions for the circuit and point-to-point choices.

## New

- **Xbox memory** (launcher, Game row): 128 MB (default) up to 1 GB. To be clear, the game itself only uses about 44 MB and never asks for more, so this doesn't make it faster today. It's room for future features.

If you have 0.1.1 or later, the launcher offers the update itself under **Updates**. After updating, it prepares the game once more, which takes about a minute.
