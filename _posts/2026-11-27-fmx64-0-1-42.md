A big one. Thanks to everyone who sent reports: several of these fixes came straight from you.

## Fixed

- **Freezes while menus load things**, for example browsing rims in Buy Upgrades. The cause was in how we convert the game's code: the original Xbox had one CPU core, and on a multi-core PC two of the game's threads could corrupt a shared count, after which the game waited forever. This affected every earlier version and could explain other rare menu freezes too.
- **Dark road in the chase cameras** on Blue Mountains, Alpine Ring and other tracks. The game uses a shadow test built into the Xbox graphics chip for the car's sun shadow, and we weren't emulating it. The road is now as bright as in the hood view, and the car casts a proper shadow.
- **Grey, patchy paint on showroom cars** (dealer, car select, the car reward screen): same cause, same fix.

## New

- **Controls:** a Controls window in the launcher to remap the keyboard and the controller. An optional "Your bindings" setting makes the on-screen button pictures show your own keys or remapped buttons. The original pad pictures stay the default.
- **Import a save:** bring your Forza Motorsport save over from an original Xbox (save folder or .zip) or from xemu (its hard-disk image), from the launcher's Repair menu. We haven't been able to test a real console save yet, so please tell us how it goes.
- **Show frame rate:** just the live FPS number in the top right corner.
- **Single Race (experimental):** turn it on in the launcher's Game row and a SINGLE RACE entry appears on the main menu. Pick any circuit or point-to-point road and any car, with everything unlocked, then choose laps, opponents and AI difficulty, and just race. Nothing is earned or recorded.
- **Bigger fields (experimental):** 16 cars on track instead of 8, in Arcade and Single Race.
- The emulated Xbox now has **128 MB of memory**, like a developer console.

If you have 0.1.1 or later, the launcher offers the update itself under **Updates**. After updating, it prepares the game once more, which takes about a minute.
