FM X64 is now a **45 MB download** (it was 122 MB), and takes 47 MB installed instead of 267 MB.

## Smaller

- The compiler that prepares the game is packed, and only unpacked while it works.
- The launcher now links the game code itself, so a 75 MB tool no longer ships.
- AMD's FSR files are unpacked the first time you use FSR 4.

## Fixed

- **A Championship Series with 16 cars damaged the saved profile.** The points of cars 9 to 16 were written into the next event's progress. A profile that was already affected is not repaired.
- **A replay saved after a 16-car race crashed the Replay Theatre.**
- Series standings showed only 9 rows with 16 cars.
- With 16 cars switched on, smaller races keep the game's own starting grid.

## Single Race is no longer experimental

It ran on every circuit and road with every car class. 16-car fields stay experimental: a series continued after closing the game loses the earlier points of cars 9 to 16, and the Drag Strip jams at the start.

If you have 0.1.1 or later, the launcher offers the update itself under **Updates**. After updating, it prepares the game once more, which takes about a minute.
