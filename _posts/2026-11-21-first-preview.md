FM X64 is the original Xbox Forza Motorsport, recompiled to run natively on Windows. This first preview has every main mode working in testing: Arcade, Free Run, Time Trial and Career, the garage, paint and decals, replays and saves.

![Three monitors as one view]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/triple_monitor.jpg)

## What's in this preview

- **Widescreen** through the game's own 16:9 mode, plus 21:9, 32:9 and 48:9 with a wider view.
- **Multi-monitor span** across mixed resolutions, with the HUD on the middle screen and bezel correction.
- **Frame rates:** 30 (original), 60, a custom cap, or unlocked. Game speed and physics stay correct at every rate.
- **VSync** on by default, with tearing allowed when it's off.
- **Higher resolution** with AMD FSR 1 upscaling and sharpening.
- **5.1 surround sound** with the game's reverb.
- **Benchmark mode:** the game's AI drives a lap on the track you pick while the frame rate is measured.

## Known limits

- Split screen, system link and Xbox Live are not supported yet.
- High frame rates in races depend on your CPU. On our test PC a race runs at well over 200 fps unlocked; a direct graphics path is being worked on next.
- The first race on each track builds its shaders once, which can cause a few short hitches.
