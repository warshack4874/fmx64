FM X64 0.1.2 adds two new graphics options. Both are off by default, so nothing changes until you turn them on.

## AMD FSR 4 upscaling

Choose **Graphics > Upscaling > AMD FSR 4**. The 3D scene goes through AMD's machine-learning upscaler, which keeps fine detail like power lines, fences and grandstand rails whole where FSR 1 breaks them up. The HUD and menus are now drawn at full window resolution, so text is sharper too.

Radeon RX 9000, 7000 and 6000 cards get FSR 4. Other cards, including NVIDIA and Intel, get FSR 3.1 automatically. It needs Direct3D 12; without it FM X64 falls back to FSR 1.

## Frame generation (experimental)

Tick **Graphics > Frame generation** (it needs FSR 4 upscaling). It doubles the pictures shown: the game's original 30 fps becomes 60 pictures a second, and 60 becomes 120. The HUD stays crisp on the generated pictures.

It adds a little input delay (about 20 ms at 30 to 60), so the 60 fps mode is still the best choice when you want the quickest response. It works best with VSync on.

## Also

The experimental "Direct" graphics path now covers most of the game's drawing. It looks identical to the default and is a few percent faster at unlocked frame rates.

If you have 0.1.1, the launcher offers the update itself under **Updates**. The download is bigger now (about 110 MB) because it includes AMD's FSR libraries.
