Thanks again to our testers: this update fixes everything they found in the paint shop and the launcher, and adds an option to make the menus fill modern screens.

## Fixed

- **Sponsor decals showing pieces of earlier logos.** Put on a logo, remove it, choose another one, and bits of the old one could show up in the new one (NGK's "SPARK PLUGS" under a JUN logo, for example). Fixed.
- **Static on painted cars.** After paint or decal work, whole panels of a car could show multicoloured noise in My Cars. Fixed.
- **Decal Select out of line in widescreen.** The logo grid was drawn a column off from the selection box, so you could end up picking a different logo than the highlighted one. Fixed, along with the same problem in the vinyl editor.
- **Stretched car in menus on ultrawide screens** (21:9 and 32:9). Fixed.
- **Launcher labels drawn twice** after pressing Play. Fixed.

## New: widescreen menus

Tick **Display > Widescreen menus** in the launcher. Instead of a 4:3 box with black bars, the menus now fill the whole screen: backgrounds and bars continue softly to the edges. Every button, panel and piece of text stays exactly where the game puts it, so nothing looks stretched. It's off by default.

If you have 0.1.1 or 0.1.2, the launcher offers the update itself under **Updates**.
